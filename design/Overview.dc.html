Ledger — earning a buyer’s trust in 150 numbers

One sourcing event — 30 corrugated box lines, five vendors, five reply shapes — Principle: AI reads, code calculates, independent checks verify, the buyer decides. The comparison grid sits at the centre of the screen.

The prototype is clickable. Click any number to open its source. All five vendors, prices, doubts and scenario totals come from one dataset.

Comparison-first. The grid is the page; the source and the analyst live in its margins. Cell state is set in type — like a well-kept ledger — and colour is spent on one thing only: a doubt.

Conversation is a full-height panel beside the grid, shared by the buyer and the VP — every question is labelled with who asked it. Scenarios — each answer is applied to the grid as a scenario tab, with its rules, excluded vendors and reasons in a strip above it. Switch between scenarios, flip table ↔ chart, export. Source — clicking any price switches the same panel to the original document, highlighted, with the arithmetic.
Good at — the daily scan. Fastest to see a whole line and a whole vendor at once; prints and exports cleanly; a calm grid. Trades off — typographic marks are subtle, so they need learning; source and conversation share one panel, so you can’t read both at once.
